## Cube prompt

Create a premium cinematic 3D product illustration for Cubeship, an open-source self-hosted deployment platform. Asset: landscape website hero / static art, no text. One spectacular precision-engineered cube suspended above a dark obsidian platform, isometric view from slightly above. Cube formed from separated layers of smoked glass and black anodized metal, glowing thin cyan edge filaments, tiny internal luminous connections, a powerful cyan energy core visible between layers, subtle magenta reflection only on one edge. Material realism, high-end architectural object photography, deliberate brutalist geometry, nuanced refraction, finely detailed manufacturing. Setting almost black navy #05070a, no stars, no city, no humans, no logos, no letters, no charts, no interface. Restrained cinematic volumetric light behind object, dark negative space around every side. Cube centered, fills 65% of frame. Broad low perspective reflection on dark floor. Image intended to blend seamlessly into a dark cyberpunk product landing page. Landscape 3:2 composition. Strong beautiful silhouette at mobile size. Use cyan #2de2e6 and occasional magenta #ff2e88, pale cool silver glints. Avoid generic wireframe cube, oversaturated purple, illustration/cartoon style, clutter and panels. Render a finished sophisticated photoreal object.

## Cluster prompt

Create a premium cinematic environmental artwork for the Cubeship product website. A striking wide panorama of a physical network of precision-engineered dark cube servers, five to seven architectural monoliths of different heights on an immense black obsidian circuit plane, connected by thin brilliant cyan light paths etched into the floor. Smoked glass, black anodized metal, subtle precise edge lighting; one small magenta edge reflection in distance. Landscape widescreen composition, very wide 3:2 or 16:9. Low three-quarter aerial architectural photography. Largest tower to right, center and left punctuated by smaller cubical nodes. Dramatic depth, beautifully realistic materials, slight cyan atmospheric mist, black navy #05070a backdrop, cyan #2de2e6 highlights, restrained magenta #ff2e88. Generous black negative space along upper quarter. Expensive product campaign art, quiet science fiction engineering laboratory. No logos, no text, no digits, no UI, no people, no city skyline, no stars, no cartoon. All modules connected intentionally, geometrical clean paths, exquisite details. This is conceptual illustration of owning your infrastructure, not a literal hardware product.
